A Catalog of Hadronic Supernova Remnants for Neutrino Followup

While neutrinos from individual Galactic SNRs are almost always too faint to be detected with current instruments, a stacked analysis may offer a possible route towards detection and would give us insight into the SNRs as hadronic accelerators.
