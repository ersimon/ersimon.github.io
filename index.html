Research

I am broadly interested in theoretical astroparticle physics with a special interest in high energy phenomenon and beyond the standard model physics!
My current research involves the search for PeV particles from supernova remnants with Professor Damiano Caprioli.

Supernova remnants (SNRs) are prominent candidates for supplying the energy density and spectrum of galactic cosmic rays observed out to PeV energies. However, SNR observations and analytical estimates of diffusive shock acceleration (DSA) struggle to produce PeV protons, leaving an open question: is there something about the acceleration process that we still don’t understand? Using a novel setup for hybrid (kinetic ions/fluid electrons) simulations, we're looking at the nonlinear relationship between cosmic ray current, amplified magnetic fields, and the diffusion coefficient to estimate the highest energy particles it is possible to produce in DSA at SNR shocks.
